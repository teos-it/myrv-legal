Contact

Write to office@teosit.com. We answer within two working days. Tell us your phone model and, if it's about your account, the email you sign in with.

My RV Pro

  • Restore a purchase: Profile → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.
  • Cancel: in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel it.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, under their rules: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play in your order history.

Sign-in

  • The email sign-in link has to be opened on the same phone where you asked for it, within one hour.
  • No email? Check your spam folder, then ask for a new link.

Map and places

  • Places come from OpenStreetMap and from My RV users. Wrong or closed? Open the place and tap Report a problem.
  • To report a review, photo or place that breaks our rules, tap … on it and choose Report. We look at reports within 24 hours.
