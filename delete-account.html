My RV – Delete your account

Petro Arva PR Računarsko programiranje TEOS IT Novi Sad

In the app

  1. Open My RV and go to Profile → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account, then Delete my account.

Without the app

Write to office@teosit.com from the email address of your account and ask us to delete it. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Delete some of your data and keep your account

  • Reviews and photos: open the spot, tap … on your review or photo, then Delete.
  • Trips, costs and reminders: open them and tap Delete.
  • Checklist items: open the checklist, tap Edit and remove them.
  • Vehicles: Vehicle → Edit vehicle → Remove this vehicle.
  • Saved spots: tap the heart again on the spot.
  • Anything else, or all of it at once: write to office@teosit.com and say what to delete. We do it within 30 days.

What is deleted with the account

Your account, profile, vehicles, tank log, costs, reminders, checklist, trips, saved spots, reviews and photos are deleted for good. Your subscription record at our payment provider (RevenueCat) is deleted too.

What is kept

  • Spots you added stay on the map without your name.
  • A minimal record of moderation decisions, linked only to a random ID, to prevent abuse.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a My RV Pro subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
