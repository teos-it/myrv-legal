Location

With your permission, the app uses your location while it is in use to show what's near you. To find nearby spots it sends a position to our servers, which use it only to answer that request and don't store it. If you turn on background alerts (Pro) and allow location "Always", the app also checks your location in the background, only after you have moved a few kilometres or stopped at a service point, to alert you about water, dump, LPG and fuel stops ahead. This is processed on your phone; to find spots ahead the app occasionally sends a position rounded to about 1 km, which isn't stored either. We don't store your location history on our servers. Trip stops and spots you add are saved because you created them.

Photos

Photos are resized and their metadata, including GPS location, is removed on your device, and again on our server. Published photos are visible to anyone using the app. Only add photos you are happy to share.

Automatic content checks

Before they are published, the text of reviews, spot names, display names and photos are sent to OpenAI's moderation service, which checks them for objectionable content. If something is flagged, a person reviews it. A flag does not by itself lead to any action against your account.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or your account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings; your data and photo files are then removed. Spots you added stay on the map without your name. We keep a minimal record of moderation decisions, linked only to a random ID, to prevent abuse.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw consent (such as location access, in your phone's settings). Write to office@teosit.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
